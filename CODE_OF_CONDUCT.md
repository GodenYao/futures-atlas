# Code of Conduct

Futures Atlas adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and applies in full;
this page summarises it and adds the project's contact and scope.

## Our pledge (summary)

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act in ways
that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Empathy and kindness toward others; respect for differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Project-specific: coordinated or automated vote manipulation in Visions discussions, and
mass-generated evidence PRs that were not checked by the person submitting them, are treated
as disruptive behaviour.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, Discussions, the ledger) and
when someone officially represents the project. Report problems to the maintainer through
GitHub's private reporting ("Report content" on the comment, or a private message to
@GodenYao). Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. Enforcement follows the
Covenant's guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
