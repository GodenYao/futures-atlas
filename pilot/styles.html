Choose a rendering style before the engine fixes

Six candidate directions from an external art-direction review (Fable 5.2, Nov 2026), plus the current pilot look for comparison. Every image on this page is our own render of the pilot scene, with props from KayKit (CC0). Each one is a quick parameter-level approximation made with the ?style= switch: palette, light, fog, tone mapping, flat or terraced terrain, prop desaturation, tree clumping and a cheap tilt-shift.
The switch does not change geometry: there is no tiered plinth, river banks, SSAO or bloom yet. Judge each direction by its colour, light and terrain language, not by finish.
Reference works are given as text links to their sources only.
